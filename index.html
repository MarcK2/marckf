MarcKFragrance

Short videos about scent, from perfume to everyday life.

MarcKFragrance is a YouTube channel about the world of smell: creations inspired by perfumes (drinks, desserts, bouquets), the science of scent, the stories behind famous perfumes and raw materials, and the smells of everyday life.

About the publishing tool

The channel uses a private, internal tool to prepare its videos: it writes scripts, assembles royalty-free footage, adds a voice-over and subtitles, then uploads the finished video to the channel through the YouTube Data API. Every upload is reviewed by the channel owner, who can cancel it in YouTube Studio before it goes public.

The tool is used only by the channel owner, on the channel owner's own YouTube channel. It does not offer any service to other users and does not access anyone else's account.

YouTube API usage

  • Upload videos to the owner's channel as private, scheduled publications.
  • Add subtitle tracks (English, French, Spanish) to those videos.
  • Set titles, descriptions, tags and the altered or synthetic content disclosure.
